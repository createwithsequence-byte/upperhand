Part one

The company

Solo Brands, Inc. Grapevine, Texas. Four brands, three reporting segments, one very stressed balance sheet.

02 Portfolio · Solo Brands, Inc. · OTCQB: SBDS

One fire brand carrying three passengers.

Segment · Solo Stove

Smokeless fire pits, Pi pizza ovens, Steelfire griddles, Windchill coolers, Infinity Flame propane

FY2025: $167.2M, down 43.8%. Q2 2026: $32.7M, down 14.7%. New products are now about half of DTC stove sales. Goodwill fully impaired in 2024.

FY2025: $122.9M, up 9.1%. The only segment that grew. Investors approached the company about buying it in April 2025 (~$200M EV). No deal. Only goodwill left on the books ($73.1M).

Segment · Watersports

Q2 2026: $15.1M, up 59% after landing at Costco. Oru's Mexico facility shut in 2026, $1.4M write-off.

Folded · IcyBreeze

Bought July 2023 (~$30M), wound down 2025, $19.9M impaired. The cooling idea came back as Solo Stove "Windchill."

Sold back · TerraFlame

Bought May 2023 ($13.2M). Manufacturing sold back to the founders for $2.5M in June 2025. Solo kept the IP and distribution.

Mission on solobrands.com: "lifestyle brands built around creating and enhancing great moments and greater memories."

The celebrity era is not just over. It is disowned. The CEO who signed it, the CFO who defended it and the CMO who ran it are all gone. Do not pitch her a famous face.

How they grow it

Nano-creator seeding whitelisted into paid, affiliates, a "Brand Reps" program, "Smokeless Steve" (@mattslyon, late 2023), sweepstakes on every launch. Everything routes to email capture.

What the community is for

What is missing

The community has no recurring reason to show up. No series, no season, no show. It is a list, not an audience. That is the gap a live or music format fills.

18 Constraints she is working inside

Small team, cut budget, personal stake, public scoreboard.

Solo Stove marketing spend was cut in Q2 2026 while stove sales fell 14.7%. Agencies are "extensions" scaled up and down. Anything she buys has to show up in the media-mix model.

Scope

CMO of the whole holding company: Solo Stove, Chubbies, Oru, ISLE. Fire pits are the biggest and the sickest. Chubbies is the one growing.

Director and officer. Bought stock the week after the second delisting. Her compensation is public and her name is on the proxy. Failure is not private.

Calendar

Speaking circuit: Harvard Grid (Mar 2025), Ad Club Boston CMO+ (Jun 2026), HATCH (Jun 2026). She is building a public thesis about AI-native marketing on a shoestring. Solo Stove is the case study.

19 How a pitch lands with her

Speak engineer. Sell the moment. Bring a number.

Do
  • Lead with the fan insight, then the mechanism, then the ask.
  • Frame it as a pilot with a kill criterion. She says "pilot, learn, adapt" unprompted.
  • Use her words: third space, moments and memories, human in the loop, community.
  • Show how it feeds the 3.8M-email community and the Facebook group, not a media buy.
  • Make it giftable. Corporate Gifting and Gift Cards are top-nav on her site.
Don't
  • Don't pitch a big-budget campaign. The budget was just cut and she runs an in-house model.
  • Don't lead with AI. She co-founded an AI advisory; she will out-AI you. Lead with craft, mention the tooling once.
  • Don't bring stock celebrity. Snoop already happened and the CEO who signed it is gone.
  • Don't send a deck first. She is Boston, Harvard, engineer. Send a one-line insight and a demo.
Openers that fit her record
  • "You built a customer movement around a product with no smoke. What is the sound of it?"
  • Fanatics-era muscle: she knows fan-personalization at scale from the inside.
  • VW-era muscle: she launched an internet radio station in 1999. Music-as-brand-utility is not new to her.
  • Rice Krispies proves she will co-brand with a consumer brand that owns a ritual. Song is a ritual.
Part four

So what

My read, clearly labeled. Where a personalized-song company fits a brand whose whole promise is the gathering.

20 Openings · Songfinch × Solo Stove

The fire is the stage. They own the fire.

01 · Fireside Sessions

A recurring UGC-first content series: Songfinch artists premiere real customers' songs around a Solo Stove.

Fits her "authentic UGC beats scripted" thesis. Fills the empty entertainment lane. Cheap to produce, endlessly clippable, and it makes the brand's own community the cast.

02 · The gift stack

Song + fire pit as one gifted moment.

Corporate Gifting and Gift Cards are top-nav on solostove.com. A bundle (Mesa + personalized song) is a Father's Day, wedding and retirement product that neither company has today. Rice Krispies shows she will co-brand around a ritual.

03 · Backyard Day and Family Day

Two owned calendar moments with no soundtrack.

National Backyard Day (March) and National Family Day (Sept) are already hers. A commissioned family song sung live at the activation is the GMA segment she was reaching for.

04 · Pilot, with a kill switch

Structure it the way she talks.

One quarter, one content series, one bundle SKU, measured in email captures and community engagement, not impressions. If it does not move her media-mix model, it dies. She will respect that more than a campaign.

Caveat: the parent is a distressed, delisted company. Any deal should be paid on delivery or prepaid. Do not extend credit to Solo Brands.
