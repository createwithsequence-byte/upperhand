02 · Goals

  5. Your team makes routine changes without calling us.
  6. Pages load in under 2 seconds on a phone with a typical 4G connection.

04 · Approach

Where you asked for advice, our advice.

Your list decides most of V0. These are the calls it leaves open, and what we would do with each.

  1. 01

    Custom where Members look, proven underneath.

    Your document rules out a site that looks off-the-shelf, and a site built from plugins usually does. Everything Members see is designed for The Echelon. Underneath, it is built from our own library of tested components on Next.js, Postgres and Vercel, which is most of why V0 costs what it does. The tools are standard, so another developer could take it over, and with no plugins there is far less to patch.

    In V0
  2. 02

    Make the portal a Progressive Web App now.

    Members add it to their home screen from the browser. It opens full screen with your icon and stays signed in. It costs nothing extra in V0, and it tells you how Members actually use a phone before you pay for an app.

    In V0
  3. 03

    Keep Connect on email, and log it at the button.

    Email is where these conversations happen anyway, as your document says. The portal records the press before the email is sent, so the log is complete even when the conversation moves to email. Your team can mark each introduction as made, in conversation or closed. That records no dollar amounts, so V0 still tracks no revenue.

    In V0
  4. 04

    Give every Ambassador a personal link.

    Anyone who requests membership through an Ambassador's link arrives with the Ambassador already recorded. For everyone else, the form asks how they heard of The Echelon. Your team confirms the answer at approval, and any later change is logged.

    In V0
  5. 05

    Store Ambassadors and Partners as real records now.

    Each Ambassador is a record of its own, linked to the Members they introduced. The Ambassador dashboard is then a new screen on existing data, which is how V0 meets your “without requiring substantial rework”. Partners get the same treatment, so Partner logins can follow the same way.

    In V0
  6. 06

    Shape the profile around your questionnaire.

    Your review process starts with a Company Partner Profile and questionnaire. We shape the profile template around it, so your team copies across only the answers meant for Members, in the same order Partners gave them.

    In V0
  7. 07

    Launch Explore with no empty shelves.

    An empty category reads as a missing one. In the portal, a category appears once it has a live Partner. The public Explore page can still show every category you plan to fill.

    Your call
  8. 08

    The fine print stays on your side of the table.

    Terms, Privacy and the Company Partner Disclaimer are legal documents, so the words come from you. We set them into the site and ask Members to accept them at enrollment, with the time recorded.

    Your call

05 · W-9s

Submitted through the portal, held somewhere built for it.

Both can be true at once. Members submit their W-9 through the portal, and a specialist service holds it. That is Path A, and it is what we recommend. If you would rather keep the forms on your own platform, Path B is a build of its own.

Path A · Our recommendation

A specialist service holds the forms.

Members open the W-9 from My Echelon. Ambassadors, who have no login in V0, get a secure link by email. Either way the form is completed with a tax-form service built for exactly this, and the portal stores only the status and a reference.

  • Encryption, access control and audits are the service's specialty
  • Many such services also offer tax ID matching and 1099 filing, if you need them
  • Admin shows each record as requested, received or verified

$1,000 to connect it, inside the ten weeks, plus the service's own per-form fees

Path B

We build secure storage into the platform.

The forms live with us, in storage kept apart from everything else, and only designated IntenseGBD administrators can open them.

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest, in segregated storage
  • Two-factor sign-in, and every view and download written to an audit log
  • Backups, plus a retention and deletion schedule you set

$4,000 as its own build after V0, or two more weeks if it runs alongside. Holding tax forms means keeping that storage secure for as long as you keep them.

Either way: no W-9 is ever an ordinary upload, and the admin record shows its status without exposing a single number from the form.

06 · Security & ownership

Protected from the first build, and yours at handover.

Standard, well-tested measures for a members-only site, and a clean handover of everything we make for you.

How V0 is protected

  • Encrypted in transit on every page, and encrypted at rest in the database
  • Members see only their own record and their own history, enforced by the database, not just the screen
  • Administrators sign in with two-factor authentication
  • Every admin change is logged: who, what and when
  • Daily backups kept for seven days, and a CSV export of your records whenever you want one
  • Rate limits and spam protection on sign-in and every form
  • No Social Security numbers or tax information stored in the portal. Path B, if you later choose it, adds its own protections

What you own

  • The website, the portal, the database and all custom code, transferred to IntenseGBD on final payment
  • The design files, the content, and all Member and Company Partner data
  • Every account, opened in IntenseGBD's name from the first week. We work as invited collaborators
  • Our underlying component library stays ours, with a perpetual, royalty-free license to you, so nothing you own depends on us
  • A written handover, so another host or developer can take it over
