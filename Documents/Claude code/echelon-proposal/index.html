01 · The project

What we're building

  1. 01

    For Members

    Most will use it on a phone. They sign in, browse Company Partners by ecosystem and category, read a profile with its Echelon Member Advantage, and press Connect. The portal sits on their home screen and keeps them signed in.

  2. 02

    For prospective Members

    They arrive from an Ambassador or a referral. The public site explains The Echelon, its Standards and its two ecosystems, and takes a request for Founding Membership in a short form, with the source and Ambassador captured on the way in.

  3. 03

    For your team

    Approve Members, build Partner profiles, see every connection and keep Constant Contact current, from an admin you run yourselves.

How we build every site

  • Mobile first

    Designed for a phone, then scaled up to the desktop.

  • Fast

    Pages load in under 2 seconds on mobile.

  • Secure

    Encrypted, with a role for each kind of user, two-factor sign-in for administrators and daily backups.

  • Accessible

    Built to WCAG 2.2 AA, so everyone can read and use it.

  • Easy to run

    An admin your team uses without a developer.

  • Yours

    IntenseGBD owns the code, the data, the design files and every account, and can move them to another host or developer.

02 · What it consists of

Scope of work

Discovery & planning
One working session on the Member journey, from the request form to the first Connect. We shape the Company Partner profile template around your profile and questionnaire, and settle the roles, the Connect email and the sitemap.
Design
One direction, carried from your logo and brand standards into every page and portal screen, designed on a phone first. We refine it with you until you are happy with it, working from one consolidated set of comments at a time.
Public website
Your navigation as written: Home, The Echelon, Explore, Our Standards, Membership and Sign In. Membership carries the Request Founding Membership form. The footer carries About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, the Company Partner Disclaimer and the copyright notice.
Member Portal
Password sign-in, the Member home, Explore by ecosystem and category, and Company Partner profiles with the Echelon Member Advantage set apart at the top. Connect records the Member, the Partner, the date and the time, then sends your introduction email with IntenseGBD copied. My Echelon shows each Member's connections, videos embed from YouTube, and the portal installs to the home screen as a Progressive Web App.
Administration
Add, edit and remove Members, Company Partners, profiles, categories, Member Advantages and page content. Approve membership requests and send enrollment, where the Member sets a password and accepts your Terms. The connection report exports to CSV, and every Member record keeps its source and Ambassador. Ambassadors are records of their own, so a later dashboard is a new screen rather than a rework.
Integrations & security
Approved Members flow into Constant Contact, email goes out from your domain, and administrators sign in with two-factor authentication. Backups and rate limits are set up before launch.
W-9s
Members open their W-9 from My Echelon, and Ambassadors get a secure link by email. A specialist tax-form service collects and holds the form, and admin shows its status without exposing anything on it. That meets both of your requirements: submitted through the platform, and not stored in the portal.
Content population
We load the first Company Partner profile with your team as the pattern. Your team loads the rest, and the page copy is yours.
Testing & launch
Testing on iPhone, Android and desktop, user acceptance testing with your team, launch at theechelonsignature.com, admin training with a short written guide, and two weeks of monitoring.

Out of V0, as you set it: revenue and transaction tracking, an Ambassador portal or login, Partners editing their own profiles, marketing automation, social features and proprietary video.

04 · Timeline

Six weeks from kickoff

Each milestone in your document is here: design approval, development, testing, content population, user acceptance testing and launch. Kickoff is within five business days of signing. The dates assume comments come back within two business days.

  1. 01

    Discovery & planning

    A working session on the Member journey and the decisions that shape the build.

    We need from you two hours, your brand standards and the Company Partner questionnaire

  2. 02

    Design

    Every page and portal screen, phone first, refined with you until you approve it.

    We need from you one decision-maker, and comments in one set

  3. 03

    Development

    The website, the portal, admin, Constant Contact, W-9s and security. A staging link updates every week.

    We need from you the Constant Contact account and the final text of your legal pages

  4. 04

    Content population

    Your team loads the Company Partners, with the first one done together.

    We need from you the launch Partners' profile information and logos

  5. 05

    Testing & user acceptance

    We test on every device, then your team uses it for real and tells us what they find.

    We need from you your sign-off that it is ready

  6. 06

    Launch

    The site replaces “Launching Soon” at theechelonsignature.com and the portal opens to approved Members.

    We need from you your first invitations, ready to send

  7. 07

    Monitoring

    Two weeks of monitoring, and fixes for anything that does not work as scoped.

    We need from you anything your team or Members notice

05 · Investment

Fee and payment terms

We estimate V0 at 60 hours, priced at our project rate of $125 an hour. The fee is fixed: if the work runs past our estimate, that is ours to absorb. It covers everything from kickoff to two weeks after launch, including the W-9 service. IntenseGBD owns everything we make.

Payment terms

Three equal payments: one at signing, one when you approve the design at the end of week 3, and one at launch.

What would change it

Work not listed here, such as a second design direction, Upperhand loading all the Partner profiles, or any of the bigger builds under After launch. We would quote it as a fixed fee before starting.

This proposal holds through October 31, 2026.
