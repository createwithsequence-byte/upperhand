20 If Upperhand takes the meeting

Talk to Céline. Price the sprint. Get paid first.

Do
  • Treat Céline as the operator. Use her vocabulary: engagement, personalization, member types, "value is hard to quantify." She wrote the framework.
  • Treat the Lovable build as the spec. It already answers "what." Sell "who joins and why," not a rebuild.
  • Scope the smallest paid thing: a two-week discovery sprint, fixed fee, paid before day one.
  • Ask who signs and who pays, by entity. MICEL has none on record in Texas.
  • Route value to the asset they brag about: the founding roster. Make the first hundred real chefs the deliverable.
Don't
  • Don't extend credit, take equity, or accept revenue share in lieu of fees. Six partners tried variants of that.
  • Don't sign a multi-phase $400K SOW on the strength of the number in the brief.
  • Don't put "two Michelin stars" or "20 years" in anything Upperhand writes. It is not true and it travels.
  • Don't hold the Stripe, Supabase or domain accounts under Upperhand's name.
  • Don't send a deck first. He opens with a story; open with a one-page SOW and a price.

22 Openings · Upperhand × The Chefs Community · the author's read

Take the call. Do not take the risk.

01 · Correct the brief before the intro

Tell the connector what the record says, in three lines, before anyone is introduced.

One star, not two. Sixteen closures, two open insolvencies, a High Court fraud claim. The connector's credibility is also on the line. This is the cheapest opening in the deck (slide 14).

02 · The prepaid sprint

A two-week discovery sprint on the existing Lovable build, fixed fee, paid up front.

Output: a member-acquisition plan and a real founding roster target, not a rebuild estimate. If the fee cannot be paid before day one, the $400K does not exist (slides 5, 6, 19).

03 · Céline as the client

Contract with the co-founder who has the track record.

She ran Yelp Frankfurt and Canva's German creator community and won a CMX award for it. The community is hers to build; the platform is a Lovable project. Make her the day-to-day and put her name on the entity (slides 7, 20).

04 · Pilot, structured the way he opens

One city, one host, one live table, thirty days.

He opens venues; make the pilot an opening. Beren's Chef's Night Series becomes the first recorded Live Table on The Pass. Paid per event, measured in members joined, killed if it does not convert (slides 10, 19).

Counterparty caveat: prepaid only, no credit, no equity, no shared accounts, no work before funds clear. Verify the contracting entity and the source of funds. Ask directly about Mascolo v Riemenschneider and the Belgian bankruptcy; the answer tells you more than the RFP. The default recommendation is to pass unless 02 clears.
