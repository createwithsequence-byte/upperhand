Dated9 September 2026 · non-binding, except clause 3
1.

Parties.

Pedersen & Houpt, P.C., a Chicago law firm of twenty-six lawyers founded in 1957 (the Firm), and the companies, owners and developers who build this city (the Client).

2.

Engagement.

Counsel to Chicago's middle market.

Deals, disputes, real estate, employment and the wealth that comes out of it. The Firm has done the Client's kind of work before, usually more than once, and says so with dates and figures in clause 5.

3.

Fees.

Fixed where the work can be scoped, hourly where it cannot, and alternative arrangements on most matters (the Fee). The Client will know the number before the work starts. The Firm has offered this since before it was fashionable.

4.

The people.

Nineteen partners, each of whom answers a direct line. The Client will work with a partner, not a team named after one.
