03 Fifteen years, three CEOs, two delistings

From Kickstarter to the pink sheets.

2011

Brothers Jeff and Spencer Jan launch the Solo Stove Lite camp stove.

2016

Bonfire fire pit on Kickstarter. $1.1M raised. The cult begins.

2021

Roll-up of Chubbies, Oru, ISLE. NYSE IPO 28 Oct at $17 (ticker DTC), ~$200M raised.

2023

Snoop "goes smokeless" in Nov. Viral, but not sales. $249M of impairments. CEO John Merris out.

2024

Chris Metz (ex-Vista Outdoor) CEO from 15 Jan. Sales −8%, net loss $180M. IcyBreeze killed.

Feb 25

Metz out after 13 months. John Larson interim CEO. Company-wide layoffs. Liz Vanzura to interim CMO.

Apr 25

NYSE halts trading, starts delisting for "abnormally low" price. Going-concern doubt in the 10-K.

Jul 25

1-for-40 reverse split. Debt refinanced ($240M term loan, JPMorgan). Relisted as SBDS. Larson permanent.

Apr 26

Delisted again: market cap under $15M for 30 days. Moves to OTCQB. Appeal withdrawn in July.

Aug 26

Q2: first quarter of EBITDA growth in the turnaround. $13.5M adj. EBITDA on $88.5M sales.

Pattern: she is an engineer who learned to sell emotion. Every quote pairs a measurable with a feeling. Pitch her the same way: a pilot with a number attached, wrapped in a moment.

19 How a pitch lands with her

Speak engineer. Sell the moment. Bring a number.

Do
  • Lead with the fan insight, then the mechanism, then the ask.
  • Frame it as a pilot with a kill criterion. She says "pilot, learn, adapt" unprompted.
  • Use her words: third space, moments and memories, human in the loop, community.
  • Show how it feeds the 3.8M-email community and the Facebook group, not a media buy.
  • Make it giftable. Corporate Gifting and Gift Cards are top-nav on her site.
Don't
  • Don't pitch a big-budget campaign. The budget was just cut and she runs an in-house model.
  • Don't lead with AI. She co-founded an AI advisory; she will out-AI you. Lead with craft, mention the tooling once.
  • Don't bring stock celebrity. Snoop already happened and the CEO who signed it is gone.
  • Don't send a deck first. She is Boston, Harvard, engineer. Send a one-line insight and a demo.
Openers that fit her record
  • "You built a customer movement around a product with no smoke. What is the sound of it?"
  • Fanatics-era muscle: she knows fan-personalization at scale from the inside.
  • VW-era muscle: she launched an internet radio station in 1999. Music-as-brand-utility is not new to her.
  • Rice Krispies proves she will co-brand with a consumer brand that owns a ritual. Song is a ritual.
20 Openings · Songfinch × Solo Stove

The fire is the stage. They own the fire.

01 · Fireside Sessions

A recurring UGC-first content series: Songfinch artists premiere real customers' songs around a Solo Stove.

Fits her "authentic UGC beats scripted" thesis. Fills the empty entertainment lane. Cheap to produce, endlessly clippable, and it makes the brand's own community the cast.

02 · The gift stack

Song + fire pit as one gifted moment.

Corporate Gifting and Gift Cards are top-nav on solostove.com. A bundle (Mesa + personalized song) is a Father's Day, wedding and retirement product that neither company has today. Rice Krispies shows she will co-brand around a ritual.

03 · Backyard Day and Family Day

Two owned calendar moments with no soundtrack.

National Backyard Day (March) and National Family Day (Sept) are already hers. A commissioned family song sung live at the activation is the GMA segment she was reaching for.

04 · Pilot, with a kill switch

Structure it the way she talks.

One quarter, one content series, one bundle SKU, measured in email captures and community engagement, not impressions. If it does not move her media-mix model, it dies. She will respect that more than a campaign.

Caveat: the parent is a distressed, delisted company. Any deal should be paid on delivery or prepaid. Do not extend credit to Solo Brands.
